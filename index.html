Eras TD — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 21, 2026

Short version: Eras TD does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. It has no ads, no analytics, no account system, and no internet connection is required to play.

What data we collect

None. Eras TD is a fully offline, single-player game. We do not operate any servers that the app communicates with, and we do not receive any information about you or your device.

What's stored on your device

Your game progress (levels completed, evolution/upgrade choices, settings such as music volume) is saved locally on your device using standard platform storage (SharedPreferences on Android / equivalent on iOS). This data:

  • never leaves your device
  • is not visible to us or to any third party
  • is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app, or manually via the in-app "Reset Progress" option

Third-party services

Eras TD does not integrate any third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or tracking of any kind. The only third-party software involved is the app store you downloaded it from (Apple App Store or Google Play), which has its own privacy policy governing your purchase and download.

In-app purchases

Eras TD does not currently offer in-app purchases. If purchases are added in a future update, this policy will be updated first to describe what (if any) data that involves — most likely nothing beyond what Apple/Google already process to complete a purchase, which is covered by their own privacy policies, not ours.

Children's privacy

Eras TD does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the app collects no data at all from any user, it does not collect data from children either.

Permissions

Eras TD does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, or any other sensitive device permission. It does not require an internet connection to function.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future feature requires collecting some data — we'll update this page and revise the effective date above before that feature ships.
